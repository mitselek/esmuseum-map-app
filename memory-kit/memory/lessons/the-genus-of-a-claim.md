# The genus of a claim

**When:** a claim resists placement, or a tier is filling with things that do not behave like
its neighbours.

## Why

Tiers are not topics. They are **failure modes**: what makes this claim stop being true, and
what would reveal that it had.

## What to do

Ask how it rots.

| rots by | genus | instrument |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| a clock, and a source could contradict it | **fact** | a refutation address; age is a queue |
| going inapplicable rather than false | **lesson** | a why; no refutation address |
| being re-decided | **ruling** | on reversal, one dated line naming what replaced it |
| the counterparty acting | **obligation** | an actor, a resolution check, an expiry |

## What not to do

Do not give every item a refutation address because the schema has the field. A pattern that
no clock can falsify gets a blank `refute`, and the blank is counted, not failed.

Do not delete a reversed ruling. "Refuted is deleted" is for facts; for rulings the reversal
**is** the record -- what changed, when, on whose word.
