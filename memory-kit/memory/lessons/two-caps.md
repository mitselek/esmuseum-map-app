# Two caps, not one

**When:** proposing a cap, or wondering why a tier caps both rows and characters.

## Why

A row cap alone does not limit a file; it changes the shape of what goes in. Growth displaces
into longer rows, and a long row is worse than an extra row: it is usually two ideas wearing
one bullet, so it cannot be cited, moved or refuted as a unit.

Measured: 10 of 10 scratchpads in one team's audit had grown that way under a row cap alone.
Another home hit the same wall and described the result as page-spanning rows.

## What to do

Cap rows **and** characters, and treat the character cap as the definition of one idea:
if it does not fit, it is two records.

## What not to do

Do not raise the character cap to admit a long record. That is the displacement happening
again, one approval at a time.
